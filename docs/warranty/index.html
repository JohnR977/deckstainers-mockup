Written warranty · Sample terms

Our warranty, in plain English

If stain we applied peels or flakes off a deck floor within 2 years, or off railings, fences or siding within 3 years, we come back and fix that area for free — labor and materials.

2years

Deck floors, stairs and other walking surfaces

3years

Railings, posts, fences, siding and log walls

What's covered

  • Peeling, flaking or lifting of the finish we applied
  • Labor and materials to prep and recoat the failed area
  • Railings, posts, fences and log walls for the longer vertical term

What's not covered

  • Normal fading and wear in high-traffic spots (that is what maintenance coats are for)
  • Damage from furniture, grills, pets, planters, ice melt or pressure washing by others
  • Wood that is rotten, split or soaked by a leak or bad drainage
  • Solid stain on deck floors gets a 1-year term, because film finishes are known to wear faster on walking surfaces

Your part

  • Keep the deck swept and clear of leaf piles
  • Call us within 30 days of noticing a problem
  • Transferable once to a new owner if you sell the home

Sample warranty terms. Each Pro Partner sets their own term lengths and wording in site.config.json. Have a lawyer read it before launch.

Why is solid stain on floors different? Film finishes like solid stain are known to peel on walking surfaces. See semi-transparent vs. solid.
