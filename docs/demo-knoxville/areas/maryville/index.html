Deck staining in Maryville with a view of the Smokies

Blount County homes face the mountains, so the decks face west and south, straight into the afternoon sun.

Free estimate in Maryville (865) 555-0163

Sun is the main enemy here

A deck that faces the mountains usually faces the sun all afternoon. UV breaks finish down faster than rain. We lean toward a semi-transparent with good pigment, and away from clear sealers, which tend to quit after a season in full sun.

Tall decks on sloped lots

Hillside homes mean tall decks with long stairs. We price stairs and railings on their own lines so there's no guessing.

Around the college and downtown

Older homes near downtown and Maryville College often have porches with years of paint. We'll tell you whether yours wants stain or paint.

Best time to book in Maryville

Same as the rest of the area: September and October are the sweet spot. The driest months here on average are October (2.81 in.) and September (3.49 in.)NOAA. Fall dates fill first, so get your photos in early. Why fall works best.
