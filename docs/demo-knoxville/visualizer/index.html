Why does it look different from the can?

Stain soaks into wood, so the wood's own color shows through, especially on semi-transparent. Old gray wood, new yellow pine and red cedar all take the same stain differently. This tool keeps your wood's grain and light and dark spots, so it's closer to real than a color chip.

Is my photo private?

Yes. The image stays on your device while you work. It's only sent to us if you fill in the form to get the full-size image. See our privacy page.

Tips for a good photo

Shoot in daylight but not in harsh noon sun. Stand back far enough to get the whole deck. Sweep the leaves off first if you can. Wet boards look darker, so wait until it's dry.
