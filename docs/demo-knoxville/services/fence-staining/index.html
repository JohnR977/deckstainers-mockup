Fence Staining in Knoxville

Fences are vertical, so a good stain lasts longer on them than on a deck floor.

A wood fence is a lot of square footage, and most of it has never been sealed. Done right once, a fence stays easy to keep up.

What we do, step by step

  1. 01

    Clean

    Wash off gray, mildew and dirt so the stain soaks into wood, not grime.

  2. 02

    Protect

    Cover plants, AC units, cars and the neighbor's side if we're only doing yours.

  3. 03

    Spray & back-brush

    We spray to cover fast, then brush it in. Spray-only jobs look fine for a season and then fade unevenly.

  4. 04

    Both sides

    Priced per side. Ask your neighbor. Many split the cost of the other side.

Questions

About fence staining

How is fence staining priced?

By the linear foot and height, per side. Send a photo and the rough length and we'll give you a range.

Can my fence match my deck?

Yes, but fences and decks often look different with the same stain because the wood and sun are different. We'll test it first.
