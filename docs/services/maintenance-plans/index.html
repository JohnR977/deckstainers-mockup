Questions

About maintenance plans

What's the difference between a maintenance coat and a full job?

A full job may include stripping, heavy cleaning, brightening and two coats. A maintenance coat is a gentle wash and one fresh coat over finish that's still sound. It's faster and costs much less.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Yearly plans can be cancelled any time before the next visit is booked. Monthly plans can be cancelled with 30 days' notice.
