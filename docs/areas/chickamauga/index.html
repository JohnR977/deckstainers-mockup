Farmhouse porches and back decks

Wraparound porches, back decks that step down to a yard, and the odd carport or pole barn. We'll look at all of it and help you decide what's worth doing first.

Full sun, open fields

Out of the trees, a deck takes the full Georgia sun all afternoon. UV breaks down a finish faster than anything. A semi-transparent with good pigment holds up better than a clear, which can look great for a season and then quit.

Local question

Asked in Chickamauga

Why not a clear sealer? I like the natural look.

Clear finishes have almost no pigment, and pigment is what blocks the sun. In full sun they wear off quickly. A light 'toner' or a semi-transparent keeps the natural look and lasts much longer.
