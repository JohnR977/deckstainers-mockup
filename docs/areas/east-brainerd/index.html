The builder deck that never got stained

The most common thing we see here: a pressure-treated deck the builder left bare. After a few summers it's gray, rough, and starting to split. It's not too late. A cleaning, a brightener, and the right stain bring most of them right back.

HOA colors

If your neighborhood has an approved color list, send it to us. We'll match it, and you can try the colors on your own deck photo in our visualizer before you send the request to your HOA.

Same street, same week

When we do one deck in a subdivision, two or three neighbors usually ask. If you get neighbors together for the same week, ask about a neighborhood discount.

Best time to book in East Brainerd
