Privacy

Privacy policy

Short version: we use your info to give you a price and do the job. We don't sell it.

Demo site: forms on this site save to your own browser only (localStorage) so the dashboard can show them. Nothing is sent to a server. Clear your browser data to remove it.

What we collect

When you ask for an estimate, use the calculator, or use the color visualizer and ask us to send the result, we collect what you type in (name, phone, email, address, deck details) and any photos you upload.

How we use it

  • To give you a price and schedule your job
  • To text or email you about your job
  • To remind you when your deck is due for maintenance, if you're a customer
  • To ask for a review after your job, once

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent your information. We don't add you to marketing lists you didn't ask for.

The color visualizer

Your deck photo is processed in your own browser. It is only sent to us if you fill in the form to get the full-size image.

Cookies and analytics

This site doesn't use advertising cookies. The demo uses your browser's local storage to hold form entries for the dashboard.

Questions or removal

Email hello@deckstainers.example or call (423) 555-0142 and we'll delete your information.
